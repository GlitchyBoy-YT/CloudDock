COMMON QUESTIONS

Quick answers.

Is CloudDock available right now?+

No. CloudDock is in development. Hosting orders, customer accounts, and production services are not available through this preview.

Will hosting be free?+

Yes. All planned hosting services are free until further notice. Future availability will still depend on infrastructure capacity and clearly documented fair-use limits.

Can I create a customer account?+

Not yet. The dashboard is a visual preview and does not accept credentials or connect to customer records.

How do I report an issue?+

Use GitHub Issues and include steps to reproduce, expected behavior, and what happened. Never post passwords, tokens, private keys, or private personal information.

How do I request help with a running server?+

CloudDock does not operate customer hosting instances yet. Service support workflows will be announced when services become available.

Where can I follow progress?+

Follow the repository and review the public roadmap and status page for development updates.
