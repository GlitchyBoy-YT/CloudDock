THE BUILD PLAN

From preview to platform.

A transparent view of the major milestones CloudDock needs to complete. These are project goals, not delivery-date promises.

01
IN PROGRESS

Public website & product direction

Build the responsive website, product pages, pricing policy, documentation, support information, and customer dashboard preview.

  • Public pages and navigation
  • Responsive layouts and theme support
  • Product and free launch policy
02
FOUNDATION PREPARED

Database & account foundation

Prepare a secure data model and connect authentication only after the project environment and policies are configured.

  • Initial Supabase schema and row-level security
  • Customer registration and sign-in
  • Profile and session management
03
PLANNED

Customer service management

Turn the dashboard preview into a real workspace backed by authenticated customer records.

  • Service request workflow
  • Customer activity and notifications
  • Staff review tools and audit history
04
PLANNED

Hosting infrastructure

Select providers and build trusted server-side provisioning workflows for the first supported product category.

  • Infrastructure provider evaluation
  • Provisioning and lifecycle automation
  • Resource limits, backups, and monitoring
05
PLANNED

Public launch readiness

Complete operational, security, support, and documentation requirements before opening services.

  • Verified status monitoring
  • Support and incident processes
  • Final terms, privacy, and fair-use documentation
Roadmap notes

Milestones may change as development progresses. CloudDock's planned services remain free until further notice. No launch date or production availability is being promised.
